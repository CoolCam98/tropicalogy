Two main directions — a private get-together or a workplace gathering — but the spirit is the same either way.

Parties & Celebrations

Birthdays, housewarmings, showers, or just an excuse to get friends together — a plant event makes for a memorable afternoon and sends everyone home with more than a hangover. It's hands-on without being demanding, works for a wide range of ages, and gives a gathering a built-in centerpiece.

Team Building & Workplace Events

A genuinely different kind of team activity — collaborative, low-pressure, and a real break from the screen. Great for offsites, staff appreciation, onboarding, or marking a milestone. Everyone makes something together and walks away with a plant for their desk and a shared afternoon worth remembering.

Something Else Entirely

Have an occasion that doesn't fit neatly into either box? That's completely fine. We're happy to build an event around whatever you're planning — the format is flexible, and the best events often start with an idea we haven't done before.
