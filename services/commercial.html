Discuss Your Project

Different rooms,
different problems to solve.

"Commercial" covers a lot of ground. Here's how the thinking shifts depending on the kind of space we're working in.

Offices & Workplaces

The goal in a workplace is usually calm and focus — greenery that makes the space feel better without adding to anyone's plate. We place plants where they'll soften sightlines and break up open-plan sprawl, using planted dividers and desk-adjacent greenery to do that quiet work. The selection is built to suit the space and the way it's used.

Lobbies & Reception

This is the first impression, so it can carry more drama. Larger specimens, sculptural forms, and statement containers set a tone the moment someone walks in. Lobbies often have generous light from glass facades, which opens up species choices — but that same glass brings glare and heat we design around.

Restaurants, Cafes & Lounges

Hospitality spaces want atmosphere — warm, alive, memorable — but they're also tough environments, with heat, grease, tight service paths, and long hours. We choose plants that hold up to all of it and style them so they read as part of the room's design, not an afterthought near the window.

Retail & Storefronts

In retail, greenery supports the brand and keeps customers in the space longer. Plantings need to frame product without crowding it and survive a sales floor's realities. We design displays that feel intentional and shift with seasonal merchandising when that matters.

Healthcare & Wellness

Waiting rooms, clinics, and wellness spaces benefit enormously from a sense of calm. A thoughtful planting can make a room that often feels clinical feel noticeably more human and welcoming — and we design it to suit the realities of how that kind of space is used.

Specialty & Large-Scale

Airports, public spaces, atriums, propagation walls, themed builds — the projects that don't fit a category. These are where we get to be most ambitious: diverse tropical ecosystems, big architectural moments, installations that become a landmark inside the building.

Also for teams

Corporate Gifting

A plant on every desk.

Not every commercial project is an installation — sometimes it's getting greenery into the hands of an entire team.

It's a versatile option: office-wide rollouts that bring a whole floor to life at once, holiday and client gifts, new-hire welcome kits, employee appreciation, or marking a milestone or office opening. Each plant is paired with a styled container and chosen to suit the kind of light a typical desk gets — a low-effort way to put greenery in a lot of hands at the same time.

The install is the
easy part.

Our maintenance service exists so your plants are never something you have to think about. On a recurring schedule, we handle watering and pruning, check the health of every planting before problems become visible, manage pests early, rotate seasonal color where it makes sense, and refresh or reposition anything that isn't performing.
