Ways we can
help at home.

From a full design to a second opinion on the plants you already own — pick the level of help that fits where you are.

Design & Styling

The core of what we do — choosing the right plants for your rooms and your light, then styling them with containers that suit your home. We think about how a planting sits in a space: scale, placement, the way greenery softens a room or draws the eye. The result is a home that feels intentional, not accumulated.

Custom Herb Gardens

A kitchen herb garden built for the spot you have — a windowsill, a counter, a sunny corner — and designed to be genuinely usable, not just decorative. We set it up with the right herbs for your light and walk you through keeping it productive, so fresh herbs become part of how you cook.

Plant Health Checks

Already have a collection? We can take a look at it. A health check is an honest assessment of what's thriving, what's struggling, and why — whether something needs a new spot, a different container, or just a change in routine. It's the fastest way to turn a frustrating collection into a happy one.

Educational Visits

For the people who want to get genuinely good at this. A visit can cover whatever you want to learn — reading your home's light, watering with confidence, spotting and handling pests early, propagation, repotting. You come away understanding your plants, not just following instructions.

Maintenance Plans

If you'd rather not think about upkeep at all, we offer ongoing care for the home too. Regular visits to keep everything healthy and looking its best — the same steady attention we bring to commercial work, scaled to a house. Ideal for larger collections, busy households, or anyone who loves the look but not the routine.
